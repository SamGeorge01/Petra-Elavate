About Petra Elevate

Named for stone. Built for altitude.

Petra Elevate started in 2019 with a simple observation: the New Zealand businesses doing the best work were too often presented like the least impressive ones. A five‑star architecture practice with a template website. A hospitality group with a beautiful room and an unremarkable Instagram grid. The craft was there. The perception wasn't catching up.

We're a small studio by design — strategists, designers, developers and writers who work across branding, web and growth as one discipline rather than three separate vendors. Every project starts from the same question: what does this business need people to believe, before they've set foot inside?

We work with a limited number of clients at a time, mostly across hospitality, real estate, architecture and construction, alongside international clients who want a New Zealand studio's clarity and craft.

  • Mission

    Make credibility visible.

    Translate real quality of work into a brand and digital presence that reads as trustworthy in the first three seconds.
  • Vision

    New Zealand craft, held to an international bar.

    To be the studio international clients call when they want local insight without lowering their standards.
  • Values

    Transparency, craftsmanship, measured results.

    We show our working, sweat the details others skip, and tie our work to numbers you can check.
  • Why us

    One team, one accountable thread.

    Strategy, design, development and growth sit under one roof, so nothing is lost in the handover between agencies.

Questions

Before you book a call.

How long does a typical website project take?+

Most website projects run 6–10 weeks from signed proposal to launch, depending on scope and how quickly content and feedback come back to us. Branding‑plus‑website engagements typically run 10–16 weeks.

How is pricing structured?+

Every project is quoted as a fixed price after discovery, not billed hourly. You'll see the full breakdown before signing off, and ongoing work — SEO, marketing, support — is billed as a clear monthly retainer.

How many rounds of revisions are included?+

Design and development each include two structured review rounds. Additional rounds can be added at an agreed rate — we'd rather quote that upfront than let scope drift silently.

Do you handle SEO for existing websites, not just new builds?+

Yes. We run a technical and content audit on any existing site, then propose a scoped plan — you're never required to rebuild the site to work with us on SEO.

What happens after launch?+

Every build includes a 30‑day post‑launch support window as standard. Most clients then move to a monthly maintenance or growth retainer, though this is never a requirement.
